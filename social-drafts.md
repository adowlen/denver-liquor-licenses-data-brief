## LinkedIn draft (company page, for a future Friday synthesis)

Five neighborhoods hold 28.2% of Denver's active liquor licenses.

The city's liquor license register lists 2,215 records. Of the 1,965
licenses in force, 554 sit in Five Points, Union Station, Highland,
the Central Business District, and DIA - neighborhoods that hold
7.4% of the city's residents. The CBD records 20.7 active licenses
per 1,000 residents against a citywide rate of 2.7, and one
neighborhood, Indian Creek, records none at all.

The register also shows its own seams: 27 active licenses carry
placeholder coordinates and no neighborhood, and its issue-date
field starts in 2012, so it cannot say which licensed premises are
genuinely old.
